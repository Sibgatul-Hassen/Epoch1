FIELD GUIDE

User manual.

Listen for the first time

Choose Play preview to audition the featured soundscape. Open studio or select a preset card to shape a full composition. Sound starts when you press Play.

Follow the tree

Each branch divides a stretch of musical time. Its split places a note. The glowing path traces the chain of recursive calls that produced it. Softer, finer tips answer the larger branches.

Compose and repeat

Choose a mood for a scale, instrument and atmosphere. Depth adds generations, branching rhythm moves the splits, and tempo sets the pace. Space changes the room sound. Ambience mixes in tide, wind or rain. Turn Musical pulse off for freer timing. Keep the mood, seed and settings to recreate a soundscape.

Control playback and export

Play and Pause hold your place. Stop rewinds. Drag the timeline to seek or enable Loop for a crossfaded repeat. The speaker slider sets listening volume. WAV export uses the mastered composition level. Export WAV downloads the full stereo composition, including its ending.

Keyboard and motion

Press Space in the studio to play or pause when focus is outside a control. Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move, Enter or Space to activate buttons, and arrow keys to adjust sliders. Escape closes this guide. Automatic motion pauses when you hover or focus the gallery. Your device’s reduced-motion preference starts it paused.
